This is a website for you to learn the basics of Portuguese (Portugal accent) through repetition of the most common verbs.

Why?

A faster than usual way to learn a language is through exposure by watching, listening to or reading content in the language(1). However, to be able to understand any of it, the learner has to have a basic understanding of the language to start consuming the content. A large quantity of Portuguese nouns are easily understood by English speakers. However, the absolute majority of the verbs, especially common ones, are different from English, making them the main roadblock to comprehension. Therefore, it is essential to learn the most 400 most common words in Portuguese to be able to continue learning.
